# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to contribute regardless of background or experience. This
applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions, accept corrections, and respect different levels of experience.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or post sexual content or personal attacks.
- Do not publish another person's private information without their permission.
- Avoid spam and keep discussions relevant to the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, or restrict participation
when behavior violates these expectations. Responses should reflect the severity
and repetition of the behavior; participants may be asked to correct their conduct
before further action is taken.

Use GitHub's **Report content** controls to report abuse privately to GitHub. For
ordinary moderation needs, ask the maintainer in the affected thread without
repeating harmful content or disclosing private details. This project does not
currently offer a separate private conduct-reporting mailbox.
